The problem

Many traffic violations happen where monitoring doesn't reach.

Fixed cameras and patrols cover only part of the road network. Dangerous behaviour on side roads, in residential areas and at changing locations can go unrecorded.

  • Limited coverage. Fixed infrastructure is costly to install and is tied to specific locations.
  • Unstructured public reports. Citizens who witness violations often have no reliable, verifiable way to submit evidence.
  • Review effort. Evidence that does arrive must still be checked, organised and assessed by qualified people.
Our solution

Citizens submit. AI assists. Government decides.

A structured, verifiable path from a citizen's smartphone to an authorized government reviewer.

Verified citizens

Capture evidence

Identity-verified users photograph or record potential violations and submit them through the app.

AI assistance

Organise and analyse

AI helps process submissions, including licence-plate recognition, so reviewers receive structured, consistent case files.

Authorized personnel

Review and decide

Government reviewers assess each submission and keep full control over any enforcement decision.

The company does not issue fines. Enforcement decisions rest solely with the participating authority.

How it works

Four steps from road to review.

  1. Verify

    Citizens register and complete identity verification before they can submit reports.

  2. Report

    A photo or video of a potential violation is captured and submitted from a smartphone.

  3. Process

    AI assists with analysing the evidence and recognising the vehicle's licence plate.

  4. Review

    An authorized reviewer accepts or rejects the report. Eligible accepted reports may earn a reward where an authority agreement allows.

Value

Designed for authorities and for the public.

For government

  • Additional evidence from locations beyond conventional monitoring coverage.
  • Structured review workflows in a dedicated portal, with AI-prepared case information.
  • Configurable deployment for different countries, jurisdictions and reporting rules.
  • Authority stays in control of every enforcement decision.

For citizens

  • A direct way to contribute to safer roads in their community.
  • A simple smartphone workflow for submitting photo and video evidence.
  • Potential financial rewards for eligible accepted reports, under agreements with participating authorities.
  • Transparent outcomes, with each report reviewed by authorized personnel.
Employment opportunity

The platform could create flexible or remote evidence-review roles for qualified former or retired officers, applying their experience to support authorized review teams.

Any such roles would exist only where the participating authority permits and authorizes them.

  • Flexible and remote arrangements
  • Subject to authority permission
